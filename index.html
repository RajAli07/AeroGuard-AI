    How the engine decides

    The first 30 s are a calm calibration ride. The engine learns that rider's normal brake, steering and lean statistics, then scores each second by how far the last 5 s deviate, plus speed and lean at speed. Scores are smoothed and use hysteresis to avoid flicker. A crash needs impact above 4 g, roll above 60 degrees and 2 s of stillness. This is a risk detector, not an accident predictor.
